Welcome to SiteClock

Your time, across every visit.

Your first clock is ready

Open a website to start tracking. The corner timer shows your total time; hover over it to see today and the last 7 days. Refresh any website tabs that were already open when you installed SiteClock.

Only the selected tab in your focused Chrome window counts. Tracking pauses when you leave Chrome, lock your computer, or reach 30 minutes without input.

Choose where to keep your time

Use SiteClock on this computer without an account, or choose cloud backup. Recovery-code and unencrypted backup combine site totals across computers; device-only encryption keeps site names separate to each Chrome profile.

Choose how website names are protected in cloud backup

This includes existing, unassigned totals on this installation. Page contents, full URLs, and active-tab information are not uploaded. You can pause backup or delete cloud history in settings. Google handles sign-in; SiteClock never receives your Google password.

Save your recovery code

This code is the only way to unlock encrypted site names on another computer if this Chrome profile is unavailable. SiteClock cannot recover it.
