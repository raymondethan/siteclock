Accounts and cloud backup

Keep your time across computers

Sign in with the same Google account on each computer to back up your website totals and see your combined time.

Checking account…

Off by default for each installation. Enabling uploads this installation’s existing, unassigned website totals. While paused, new usage stays local and catches up when re-enabled. Previously assigned account history stays separate.

Only hostnames and cumulative durations are backed up. Active tabs, page contents, and full URLs stay off the server. The extension never receives your Google password.

Delete cloud history

This deletes usage across all installations and pauses backup. Your account remains. Existing local contributions are kept locally and will not automatically upload again. Other installations pause when they next connect.

Deleted data may remain in disaster-recovery backups for up to seven days. Recovery procedures must preserve your deletion.

Active browsing only. Local tracking continues without an account or a connection.
