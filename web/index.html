Count the time you’re there

Tracks the selected tab in the focused Chrome window. Pauses when you leave Chrome, lock your computer, or reach 30 minutes without input.

Keep it on this computer

Start without an account. Your website totals stay in your Chrome profile, across browser restarts.

Or take your time with you

Coming in the next release: choose Google sign-in and enable backup to restore your time or combine totals across computers. Backup is optional on every installation.

Ready in a few clicks

  1. Install SiteClock from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Start using it locally. Cloud backup onboarding arrives in the next release.
  3. Open a website—or refresh an existing tab—and your clock starts.

Your browsing deserves privacy

The current store version keeps your website totals on your computer. The next release adds optional cloud backup with encrypted hostnames and a recovery key (recommended), or unencrypted backup. Existing accounts configured for profile-only encryption keep that setting. Dates and durations remain readable to the service in every mode. SiteClock does not save page contents, full URL paths, passwords, or individual keystrokes. No ads or third-party analytics.
