Count the time you’re there

Tracks the selected tab in the focused Chrome window. Pauses when you leave Chrome, lock your computer, or reach 30 minutes without input.

Keep it on this computer

Start without an account. Your website totals stay in your Chrome profile, across browser restarts.

Or take your time with you

Choose Google sign-in and enable backup to restore your time or combine totals across computers. Backup is optional on every installation.

Ready in a few clicks

  1. Install SiteClock from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Choose local use or explicitly enable cloud backup.
  3. Open a website—or refresh an existing tab—and your clock starts.

Your browsing deserves privacy

Cloud backup is optional. Choose encrypted hostnames with a recovery code, encryption kept on this Chrome profile, or unencrypted backup. Dates and durations remain readable to the service in every mode. SiteClock does not save page contents, full URL paths, passwords, or individual keystrokes. No ads or third-party analytics.
